Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before they ship their first page. If yours is not here, the team answers within a working day.

Do I need a build step?
No. Components are plain HTML with data attributes and a stylesheet; drop them into any page, static or server-rendered.
Can I use my own design tokens?
Yes. Every colour, space and radius resolves from a custom property, so a theme is one stylesheet that redefines them.
Is the framework accessible out of the box?
Roles, names, keyboard behaviour and reduced-motion fallbacks are part of each component's manifest, and the audit checks a page against them.
What does it cost?
The registry is free while you build. Paid plans add hosted previews and a private component registry for your team.

Billing and plans

How seats, invoices and upgrades work. Numbered so support can point you at the right answer.

Need an invoice? Write to billing

How are seats counted?
Anyone who can edit a project is a seat. Viewers and commenters are free, without limit.
Can I change plans mid-cycle?
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
Do you offer annual billing?
Annual plans are billed up front at two months off the monthly price.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Cards and bank transfer. Purchase orders are available on annual plans.
How do I cancel?
From the workspace settings, any time. Your data stays exportable for ninety days after the last renewal.

Before you install

Which browsers are supported?
The current and previous release of every evergreen browser. Older engines get the markup and the tokens, without the animations.
Does it work with my framework?
Components are HTML, so any framework that renders HTML can render them; typed bindings are generated for the common ones.
Where do I report a problem?
Open an issue in the registry repository with the audit output attached.
